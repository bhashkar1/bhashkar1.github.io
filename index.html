01 — About

About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Department of Economic Sciences, IISER Bhopal, working at the intersection of macroeconomics, monetary policy, and financial economics.

My research examines how monetary transmission mechanisms operate under financial frictions, with particular focus on emerging market economies and the role of credit market imperfections in amplifying macroeconomic shocks.

Before my doctoral studies, I completed MA in Finacial Economics from University of Hyderabad (2022-24). I have worked as a Research Analyst at Institute of Economic Growth (IEG) under TATA Chair on IT.

02 — Research

Working Papers

2024Working Paper

Monetary Transmission Under Financial Frictions: Evidence from Indian Credit Markets

Bhashkar

This paper examines the transmission of monetary policy shocks through the banking sector in the presence of financial frictions. Using a novel identification strategy and bank-level data from India, I document significant heterogeneity in the credit channel depending on bank capitalisation. A structural DSGE model rationalises these findings.

2023Working Paper

Central Bank Communication and Asset Price Volatility in Emerging Economies

Bhashkar

I study the effect of central bank forward guidance on equity and bond market volatility across a panel of emerging market economies using high-frequency event-study methods around monetary policy announcements.
