2024 Working Paper

Monetary Transmission Under Financial Frictions: Evidence from Indian Credit Markets

Bhashkar

This paper examines the transmission of monetary policy shocks through the banking sector in the presence of financial frictions. Using a novel identification strategy and bank-level data from India, I document significant heterogeneity in the credit channel depending on bank capitalisation and liquidity ratios. A structural DSGE model with financial intermediation rationalises these findings and quantifies the amplification role of balance-sheet constraints. [Replace with your actual abstract.]

2023 Working Paper

Central Bank Communication and Asset Price Volatility in Emerging Economies

Bhashkar

I study the effect of central bank forward guidance on equity and bond market volatility across a panel of emerging market economies. Using high-frequency event-study methods around monetary policy announcements, I find that clarity of communication reduces volatility persistence but increases immediate market sensitivity.
